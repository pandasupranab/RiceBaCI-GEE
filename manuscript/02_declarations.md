## Data Availability Statement

*(Place: end of manuscript, after References, as a labeled section.)*

All datasets used in this study are openly accessible without permission, application, or institutional gatekeeping. The Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, JRC Global Surface Water, ERA5-Land, MODIS MCD12Q2, ESA WorldCover and GAUL datasets analysed in this study are openly available through Google Earth Engine. The IBTrACS cyclone track data are openly available from NOAA at <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-best-track-archive>. ICRISAT Village Dynamics in South Asia (VDSA) microdata are openly available at <http://vdsa.icrisat.org>. District-level Kharif rice yield records are openly available from the Government of India Open Data Platform at <https://data.gov.in>. The RiceBaCI-GEE source code is released under the MIT licence at <https://github.com/pandasupranab/RiceBaCI-GEE>; permanent versioned snapshots (pre-registration, submission and acceptance releases) are archived at Zenodo via the GitHub–Zenodo integration at <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20024578>. The corrected 10-m SOS/POS/EOS rasters and validation reference points generated for this study are available on Mendeley Data at <https://doi.org/[Mendeley Data DOI: pending]>. PlanetScope NICFI imagery, freely accessible to academic users worldwide via the NICFI Basemaps programme, cannot be redistributed under the NICFI programme terms; the derived validation point labels (coordinates and class labels only) are included in the Mendeley Data deposit. A complete dataset manifest with download URLs and instructions is provided in `docs/Data_Sources_Manifest.md`.
