# Abstract

**Title:** Quantisation and seasonal-boundary artefacts in Sentinel-2 rice phenology: a reproducible quality-control framework for cyclone-impact studies

Phenology metrics derived from Sentinel-2 time series — start of season (SOS), peak of season (POS), and end of season (EOS) — are increasingly used as outcome variables in causal impact studies of tropical cyclones, drought, and heat stress on rice cultivation. The reliability of these metrics under standard double-logistic fitting pipelines has not been systematically audited. We document a specific, reproducible failure mode — day-of-year (DOY) quantisation at fitting-window boundaries — that produces artefactual phenological signals indistinguishable from genuine climatic effects. Using 8 districts × 6 years of Sentinel-2 L2A 10-m NDVI dekadal composites across coastal and inland Odisha, India (n ≈ 3,500 rice pixels per district-year), we demonstrate that the pre-QC (v1.0.2) pipeline concentrates 72.7% of district-year EOS estimates at a single boundary DOY (349) and 65.6% of POS estimates at a single DOY (288); after the three-gate QC framework is applied (v2.0), these mode-shares collapse to 8.3% and 8.3% respectively, and the SOS mode-share falls from 20.3% to 12.5%. We propose a three-gate reproducible QC framework — mode-share thresholds, biological plausibility gates, and fit-quality screening — and demonstrate its application in a difference-in-differences (DiD) case study testing whether Cyclone Fani (2019), Cyclone Amphan (2020), and Cyclone Yaas (2021) detectably shifted Kharif rice phenology in five coastal Odisha treatment districts relative to three inland control districts. All three phenometrics return null effects after QC: τ_SOS = +7.6 d (p_WCB = 0.39), τ_POS = −2.3 d (p_WCB = 0.47), τ_EOS = −4.1 d (p_WCB = 0.45), with 95% confidence intervals spanning zero. The null DiD is framed as validation evidence: removing the boundary artefact eliminates false-positive treatment variance. We release the full GEE pipeline, panel data, and analysis code as RiceBaCI-GEE v2.0 (GitHub: https://github.com/pandasupranab/RiceBaCI-GEE; Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.20587316; OSF: 10.17605/OSF.IO/C4MP8).
