**Table S1. Panel-wide phenometric mode-share matrix: v1.0.2 vs. v2.0.**
For each phenometric (SOS, POS, EOS), the panel-wide mode DOY, mode-share (fraction of district-year median values assigned to the single most common DOY), and number of unique DOY values are tabulated for the pre-QC (v1.0.2) and post-QC (v2.0) panels. A mode-share > 0.20 (Gate A threshold) indicates boundary or dekadal quantisation contamination. The diagnostic finding is that all three phenometrics fail Gate A in v1.0.2 (EOS 0.727, POS 0.656, SOS 0.203) and all three pass in v2.0 (EOS 0.083, POS 0.083, SOS 0.083).
*[Source: analysis/baci_panel_real_v1.csv and analysis/baci_panel_real_v22.csv; full values reproduced in supplement.md Table S1.]*

**Table S2. Event-study coefficients: v2.0 panel.**
Full event-study results from Equation (2) for all three phenometrics and all five post-reference years (k = 1, …, 5). Columns: metric, event year index k, year, β̂_k (days), SE (cluster-robust), lower 95% CI, upper 95% CI, number of treatment districts at event year k. Reference year: k = 0 (2019, Cyclone Fani). No coefficient for any metric-year combination is statistically significant at the 5% level.

**Table S3. Event-study leads/lags with 2018 as reference year (parallel-trends evidence).**
Event-study coefficient table for SOS and POS with 2018 as the reference year. The 2017 row is the pre-period lead; 2019–2024 rows are post-period lags. Columns: metric, year, period (pre/post), β̂ (days), SE, 95% CI low/high, p-value. EOS is omitted because the v2.0 panel EOS distribution takes only two distinct values (DOY 349/350), preventing identification. The pre-period Wald test (1 d.f.) is χ²=1.80, p=0.18 for SOS and χ²=0.03, p=0.86 for POS; neither rejects the parallel-trends null at conventional levels.
*[Source: rse_final/reviewer_rebuttal/table_S10_event_study.csv]*

**Table S5. MAR vs. MNAR diagnostic for the QC framework (selection bias test).**
DiD coefficient from regressing the per-district-year QC fit-failure rate on treat × post + district FE + year FE on the full unfiltered 48-row panel. Columns: outcome, DiD coefficient, SE, 95% CI low/high, p-value, verdict, interpretation. The treat × post coefficient is −0.009 (95% CI [−0.055, +0.037], p = 0.702) — small, far from significance, and ruling out treatment-induced fit-failure-rate increases larger than ~4 percentage points. Interpreted as evidence of MAR (no selection bias).
*[Source: rse_final/reviewer_rebuttal/table_S11_mar_mnar.csv]*

**Table S8. Minimum detectable effect (MDE) at 80% power, district vs. pixel level (cluster-robust).**
Power analysis derived from post-QC cluster-robust residual standard errors of Model 1 (district panel) and Model 2 (cell-level panel), both under G = 8 district clustering. Columns: metric, τ̂ main, SE (district, Model 1), district MDE at 80% power (days), SE (pixel, Model 2 cluster-robust), pixel MDE at 80% power (days), note. Under district-clustered inference the district-level model detects effects of ≥ 22.7 d for SOS, ≥ 8.1 d for POS, and ≥ 12.9 d for EOS; the pixel-level Model 2 detects effects of approximately ≥ 13.9 d (SOS), ≥ 4.8 d (POS), and ≥ 10.2 d (EOS).
*[Source: rse_final/reviewer_rebuttal/table_S14_mde.csv]*
