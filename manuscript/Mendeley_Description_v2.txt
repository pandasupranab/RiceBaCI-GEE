Hypothesis. Cyclone-induced saline inundation contaminates the surface-water signal in Sentinel-1/2 rice-phenology retrievals over the Bay of Bengal coast, biasing apparent SOS/POS/EOS of Kharif rice. A classifier-based Before–After–Control–Impact (BACI) correction that separates cyclone-flood pixels from agronomic transplanting-flood pixels recovers the true phenological shift; the raw pipeline over-states the cyclone-attributable SOS delay.

Findings. Across 8 coastal/inland Odisha districts × 8 Kharif seasons (2017–2024), a two-way fixed-effects DiD on the district panel gives τ̂_SOS = 15.289 d (raw) → 15.108 d (v2.1 corrected); Δτ_SOS = −0.181 d (≈1.2 % attenuation). τ̂_POS: −3.587 → −3.677 d; τ̂_EOS becomes −0.239 d after restoring 20 right-censored cells. The v0.3.0 random-forest saline-flood classifier reaches OA = 0.990 (full-feature), 0.844 (SAR-only), 5-fold CV OA = 0.831. Per-district cyclone-pixel-share is bounded (Fani 0.10–2.52 %, Amphan 0.04–1.92 %, Yaas 0.42–7.21 %). Parallel-trends, wild-cluster restricted bootstrap, leave-one-out jackknife and in-space/in-time placebo tests all pass.

Data acquisition. Phenology metrics derive from Sentinel-2 SR (cloud-masked, harmonic-fit SOS/POS/EOS) and Sentinel-1 GRD VV/VH backscatter in Google Earth Engine. The n = 480 labels (cyclone vs agronomic flood) are fully programmatic: 80 from Copernicus EMS EMSR357 (Fani), 160 from UN-SPIDER S1 pre/post change-detection (Amphan, Yaas), 240 from S1 ∩ ESA WorldCover 2021 cropland ∩ JRC GSW seasonal-water in non-cyclone weeks of control years (2017, 2018, 2022–2024). Districts from GADM v4.1; ERA5 rainfall/temperature; IBTrACS tracks. Cyclones: Fani (May 2019), Bulbul (Nov 2019), Amphan (May 2020), Yaas (May 2021); Hudhud (Oct 2014, Andhra Pradesh) for transferability.

Contents. (i) baci_panel_n384.csv — 384 district-year-metric rows, raw + corrected SOS/POS/EOS in DOY. (ii) labels_panel_n480.csv — 480 labels, 8 features (S1 VV/VH pre/post, S2 NDWI/MNDWI, JRC seasonal water, ERA5 7-day precip), event provenance. (iii) rf_model_card_v0.3.0.json — full-feature and SAR-only variants with hyper-parameters, CV scores, feature importances. (iv) cyclone_pixel_share_per_district_year.csv — driver of the BACI correction. (v) did_outputs/ — static, event-study, parallel-trends, wild-cluster bootstrap, jackknife, placebo CSVs.

Interpretation and use. Keys are (district_id, year, metric); treatment = 5 coastal districts, control = 3 inland. Use the corrected SOS/POS/EOS columns for downstream phenology, yield or food-security work; raw columns are retained for replication.
